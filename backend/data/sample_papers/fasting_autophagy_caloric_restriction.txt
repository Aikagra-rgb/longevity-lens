TITLE: Autophagy Activation and Caloric Restriction Mimetics in Human Aging

ABSTRACT
Caloric restriction (CR) without malnutrition is the most thoroughly studied non-genetic intervention for extending lifespan across species. The mechanisms underlying CR include downregulation of nutrient-sensing pathways (mTOR, insulin/IGF-1), activation of energy sensors (AMPK, sirtuins), and induction of macroautophagy. This review examines human clinical trial data from the CALERIE study, time-restricted feeding (TRF) protocols, and natural caloric restriction mimetics (CRMs) such as spermidine and quercetin.

1. THE CALERIE TRIAL FINDINGS
The Comprehensive Assessment of Long-term Effects of Reducing Intake of Energy (CALERIE 2) trial demonstrated that a 14% caloric restriction over 2 years in non-obese humans led to:
- Significant reductions in cardiometabolic risk markers (hs-CRP, total cholesterol, blood pressure).
- Decreased systemic energy expenditure and basal metabolic rate beyond that expected from weight loss, indicating metabolic adaptation.
- Preservation of thymus gland volume and T-cell generation, combating immunosenescence.
- Slower biological aging as measured by Levine PhenoAge and DunedinPACE.

2. TIME-RESTRICTED FEEDING (TRF 16:8) VS CALORIC RESTRICTION
Intermittent fasting protocols—such as 16-hour daily fasting windows—confer many of the metabolic benefits of 24-hour caloric restriction without continuous energy deficit:
- Autophagy Induction: Liver and vascular endothelial cell autophagy peaks after 14–16 hours of nutrient deprivation.
- Glycogen Depletion: Shift from glucose utilization to hepatic ketone body production (beta-hydroxybutyrate), activating brain-derived neurotrophic factor (BDNF).

3. CALORIC RESTRICTION MIMETICS (CRMs)
For individuals unable to sustain caloric restriction or TRF, CRMs mimic the biochemical signal of nutrient scarcity:
- Spermidine: A natural polyamine that induces autophagy via inhibition of acetyltransferase EP300. Clinical studies show spermidine supplementation improves memory performance and lowers cardiac hypertrophy.
- Metformin & Berberine: Activate AMPK, inhibiting hepatic gluconeogenesis and mimicking CR metabolic gene expression profiles.

CONCLUSION
Activating energy-sensing networks through intermittent fasting, caloric restriction, or CRM supplementation represents an accessible approach for enhancing autophagic clearance and preserving physiological resilience.
